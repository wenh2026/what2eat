Ingredients

4 Items

Roma Tomato

2 Large

Beef Brisket

200g

Wheat Noodles

150g

Scallions

1 Bunch

Cook Mode

1

Prep & Sear

Slice the beef into bite-sized chunks. Sear in a hot pot with a dash of oil until golden brown on all sides.

2

Tomato Base

Add chopped tomatoes and stir until they break down into a rich sauce. Add water and simmer for 15 mins.

3

Finish & Garnish

Boil noodles separately, combine with the soup, and top with fresh scallions.
